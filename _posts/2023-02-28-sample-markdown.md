## 👋 Welcome

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Islamic Azad University, specializing in **Artificial Intelligence** and **Soft Computing**. With over two decades of experience in data science and computational intelligence, I focus on developing **interpretable machine learning frameworks**, **hybrid intelligence systems**, and **robust feature selection methods** for high-dimensional data.

My research bridges theoretical advances in fuzzy rough set theory with practical applications in engineering and biomedical domains. As a strong advocate for **open and reproducible science**, all my code, datasets, and preprints are publicly archived with persistent DOIs.

🔍 **Currently seeking postdoctoral opportunities** in Explainable AI (XAI), trustworthy machine learning, and reproducible scientific computing.
